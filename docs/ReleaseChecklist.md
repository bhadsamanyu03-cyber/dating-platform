# Release Checklist

No public-release criteria have been approved. This checklist is a planning shell; it must be completed by the product owner, engineering, security, operations, and legal stakeholders before a launch.

- [ ] Product scope, target market, success criteria, and owner approved.
- [ ] Feature-specific requirements and acceptance criteria approved.
- [ ] Security, privacy, legal, compliance, accessibility, and safety reviews complete.
- [ ] Production target, secrets, monitoring, backups, rollback, and incident process approved.
- [ ] Functional, integration, performance, mobile/device, and operational tests meet agreed gates.
- [ ] Support, user communication, policy, and release ownership are ready.

## Open Questions

- Who can approve a release and who can halt it?
- What criteria distinguish internal, beta, regional, and public releases?
- What rollback, customer communication, and post-release review requirements apply?
