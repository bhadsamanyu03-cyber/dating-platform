# Product Requirements

## Status and authority

This document is the product-specification entry point. It records confirmed scope and separates it from decisions reserved for the product owner. A future implementation must not treat an open question as a requirement.

## Confirmed product context

The intended product category is a public consumer mobile dating application. Its current implemented foundation is platform infrastructure and identity/authentication only. Product-domain features have not been approved or specified.

## Vision, goals, and philosophy

The product vision, target outcome, audience segmentation, differentiation, and commercial model are not yet defined by the product owner. Product philosophy must be established before product-domain implementation begins.

Engineering philosophy is confirmed: production quality, maintainability, security, explicit ownership, testability, and configuration-driven operation take priority over speed of feature delivery.

## Scope and MVP boundary

The only confirmed functional scope is account identity: registration, login, session management, email verification, password recovery/change, and soft account deletion. No profile, discovery, matching, messaging, media, feed, moderation, notification, billing, or administration behavior belongs in the current MVP boundary until specified.

## Success metrics

No product success metrics have been approved. Engineering health metrics may be collected only after their purpose, ownership, retention, and consent basis are decided.

## Open Questions

- What user problem and outcome define success?
- Which markets, languages, age groups, and platforms are in initial launch scope?
- What differentiates this product from alternatives?
- Which product capabilities constitute the first marketable MVP?
- Which measurable outcomes, targets, and review cadence define success?
