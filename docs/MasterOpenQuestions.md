# Master Checklist of Unanswered Product Decisions

Every item below requires a product-owner decision before implementation. This is the authoritative unresolved-decision register.

## Authentication

- [ ] Is email verification mandatory before product use?
- [ ] Are social sign-in, passkeys, MFA, recovery, age/identity checks, or account restoration required?
- [ ] What session lifetime, device trust, suspicious-login, and support-recovery policies apply?

## Profiles

- [ ] What profile fields, visibility, completion rules, and edit history are required?
- [ ] What bio limits, pronouns, relationship intent, and demographic choices are appropriate?

## Discovery

- [ ] What eligibility, ranking, filters, location behavior, preferences, and exhaustion behavior apply?

## Matching

- [ ] What action creates a match, and when does it expire, unmatch, or reappear?

## Messaging

- [ ] When may people message, and what deletion, delivery, read-receipt, attachment, and retention behavior applies?

## Media

- [ ] Are media types allowed, and what limits, processing, privacy, review, and deletion rules apply?

## Moderation

- [ ] What reporting, blocking, review, escalation, enforcement, appeal, and safety policies apply?

## Privacy

- [ ] What data is collected; what consent, retention, export, correction, deletion, and visibility controls apply?

## Notifications

- [ ] Which notifications exist, through which channels, with what consent, scheduling, and opt-out controls?

## Infrastructure

- [ ] What reliability, capacity, observability, backup, recovery, secrets, and incident targets are required?

## Deployment

- [ ] What cloud, regions, environments, release gates, rollback, and ownership model are approved?

## Analytics

- [ ] What events, metrics, tools, consent, retention, access, and experiment policy are approved?

## Admin

- [ ] Which internal roles, permissions, workflows, audit requirements, and support tools are needed?

## Billing

- [ ] Is monetization in scope? If so, what products, taxes, refunds, entitlement, and provider requirements apply?

## Compliance

- [ ] Which jurisdictions, age rules, privacy laws, consumer-protection rules, records, and review processes apply?
